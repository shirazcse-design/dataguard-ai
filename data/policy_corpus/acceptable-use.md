# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes the acceptable use of Harbourline devices, accounts, networks and services.

## 2. Company devices and accounts

Company devices and accounts are provided for business use. Limited personal use is permitted if it
does not interfere with work, does not break the law and does not expose the company to risk.

## 3. Email and messaging

Employees must not configure automatic forwarding of company email to personal email accounts.
Company messaging tools must not be used to share passwords.

## 4. Software installation

Only software available from the company software portal may be installed on company devices.
Requests for other software are reviewed by IT and Information Security.

## 5. Cloud services and storage

### 5.1 Approved storage

The company's managed OneDrive and SharePoint are the approved storage locations for company work.

### 5.2 Personal devices

Personal devices may access company email only after enrolment in the company mobile device
management (MDM) service.

### 5.3 Personal cloud storage

Employees may store Internal information in a personal cloud-storage account when working
remotely, provided the account is protected with multi-factor authentication. Confidential and
Restricted information must never be stored in personal cloud storage.

## 6. Monitoring

Harbourline may monitor the use of company devices, accounts and networks, within the limits of
applicable law, to protect its information and systems.

## 7. Reporting loss

Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to the IT Service Desk within 24 hours.
