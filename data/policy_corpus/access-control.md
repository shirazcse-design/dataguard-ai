# Access Control Standard

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines how access to Harbourline systems and data is requested, approved, reviewed
and removed.

## 2. Principles

Access follows least privilege and need to know. Segregation of duties must be maintained so that
no single person can both request and approve their own access.

## 3. Access requests

### 3.1 Standard access

Access is requested through the Identity portal and approved by the requester's line manager.

### 3.2 Confidential and Restricted systems

Access to systems holding Confidential or Restricted information also requires approval from the
data owner of that system.

### 3.3 Privileged access

Privileged (administrator) access is granted just in time through the privileged access management
tool, for sessions of at most 8 hours, and must be approved by the system owner and Information
Security.

### 3.4 Production customer databases

Access to production databases containing customer data is time-bound, for a maximum of 90 days.
Renewal requires a new approval; access is never granted permanently.

## 4. Access reviews

Access to Restricted systems is reviewed quarterly by the data owner. Access to Confidential
systems is reviewed every six months.

## 5. Leavers and movers

All access is removed within 24 hours of an employee leaving. When an employee changes role, their
access is reviewed within 5 business days and access no longer needed is removed.

## 6. Authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for all remote access and for every privileged account.
