# Incident Response Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how Harbourline reports, handles and learns from information security
incidents.

## 2. Reporting an incident

Any suspected security incident, including suspected data loss, must be reported to the Security
Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery, through the security hotline or the incident portal.
Employees must not investigate a suspected incident themselves.

## 3. Severity levels

Incidents are rated from Severity 1 (critical: confirmed loss of Restricted information or a major
service outage) to Severity 4 (low: a policy violation with no data exposure). Severity 1 incidents
are escalated to the Chief Information Security Officer immediately.

## 4. Response phases

Every incident follows the phases of containment, eradication, recovery and a lessons-learned
review. The lessons-learned review is completed within 10 business days of closing a Severity 1 or
Severity 2 incident.

## 5. Data breach notification

The Chief Privacy Officer assesses whether a breach of personal information must be notified.
Where notification is required, the relevant regulator is notified within 72 hours of the breach
being confirmed, and affected customers are notified without undue delay.

## 6. Evidence preservation

Systems involved in an incident must not be powered off or wiped without instruction from the
Security Operations Center, and relevant logs must be preserved.
