# Data Retention Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how long Harbourline keeps records.

## 2. Retention schedule

### 2.1 Customer financial records

Customer financial records are retained for 10 years after the end of the customer relationship.

### 2.2 Employee records

Employee personnel records are retained for 7 years after employment ends.

### 2.3 Security logs

Security logs are retained for 90 days.

## 3. Legal hold

Records subject to a legal hold must not be deleted.
