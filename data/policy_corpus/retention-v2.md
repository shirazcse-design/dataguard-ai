# Data Retention Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets how long Harbourline keeps records and how they are disposed of at the end of
their retention period. This version replaces version 1.0.

## 2. Retention schedule

### 2.1 Customer financial records

Customer financial records, including account statements and transaction histories, are retained
for 7 years after the end of the customer relationship.

### 2.2 Employee records

Employee personnel records are retained for 6 years after employment ends.

### 2.3 Security logs

Security logs are retained for 1 year in searchable storage and for 2 years in total.

### 2.4 Email

Email is retained for 3 years unless it is subject to a legal hold.

## 3. Legal hold

A legal hold issued by the Legal department overrides the retention schedule. Records under legal
hold must not be deleted until Legal releases the hold.

## 4. Disposal

At the end of the retention period records are securely deleted. Physical media is destroyed by an
approved contractor, who provides a certificate of destruction.
