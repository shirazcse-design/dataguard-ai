# Third-Party Data Sharing Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the conditions under which Harbourline information may be shared with third
parties such as vendors, partners and service providers.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to any sharing of Confidential or Restricted information with an organisation
outside Harbourline. Public information may be shared without these controls.

## 3. Pre-sharing requirements

### 3.1 Vendor security assessment

A third party must pass a Vendor Security Assessment before it receives any Confidential or
Restricted information. The assessment is repeated every 12 months while sharing continues.

### 3.2 Contract

A Data Processing Agreement must be signed before sharing begins. It must include confidentiality
obligations and a requirement to notify Harbourline of a breach within 72 hours.

## 4. Approvals

### 4.1 Confidential information

Sharing Confidential information, including confidential customer information, with a third party
requires approval from the data owner after the Vendor Security Assessment has been passed.

### 4.2 Restricted information and PHI

Sharing Restricted information requires approval from both the data owner and the Chief Privacy
Officer. Sharing protected health information (PHI) additionally requires a signed Business
Associate Agreement, and only the minimum necessary PHI may be shared.

## 5. Transfer methods

Information must be transferred using the company managed file transfer service or another
approved secure transfer method. Restricted information must never be sent as an email attachment
to a third party.

## 6. Third-Party Data Register

Every approved sharing arrangement must be recorded in the Third-Party Data Register, including the
data categories shared, the approvers and the review date.
