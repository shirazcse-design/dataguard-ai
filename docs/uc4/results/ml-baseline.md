## Why the numbers look this way: template memorisation

Documents in a scenario family are variations of one template. The same model, three ways of measuring it on TRAIN:

| measurement | level macro-F1 | category macro-F1 |
|---|---|---|
| in sample | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| random kfold leaky | 0.984 | 0.997 |
| grouped kfold honest | 0.312 | 0.143 |

In-sample and random-fold scores are near perfect because the model has seen each template; grouped folds (an unseen family) are the honest estimate and are far lower. **The model largely learns family identity, not sensitivity**, because there are only 76 training families. Any evaluation that lets a family span train and test would have hidden this.
